Our story

What began as a small home kitchen passion for baking has grown into a beloved local bakery serving the Banashankari and Girinagar community. We believe a good cake isn't just dessert — it's the centre of every celebration, big or small. That belief drives us to use quality ingredients, bake in small fresh batches, and treat every order like it's for our own family.

From birthday truffle cakes to everyday breads and cookies, everything is prepared with the same care. And because we're a neighbourhood bakery, we deliver fast — freshness is only real if it reaches you quickly.
